Every coding agent you run, in one terminal.

Force keeps your Claude Code and OpenCode sessions running on your machine or a server, whether a terminal is open or not, and tells you which one needs you.

$ curl -fsSL https://zalandemeter.github.io/install.sh | sh

Then type force. Linux, x86-64. No root, no packages, no config files.

Every screen above is Force itself, captured as text from running sessions, not a screenshot.

Close the terminal. Force keeps watch.

◐They keep running
Close the terminal, log out, lose the connection: the agents carry on. A crashed one restarts with its conversation.
!You see who needs you
A permission prompt or a question anywhere shows in the sidebar and as a count at the top. One click takes you there.
●Your limits in view
How much of your five-hour and weekly limits you have used, and when each resets, under the list.
○Every machine
force host add puts a server's sessions one click away from your laptop's.
○Any terminal, your phone
Force paints its own theme in any terminal or ssh login, and every session is reachable from the Claude app under its Force name.
◌The sessions you already have
force import takes over an earlier conversation, history and all.

The commands you will use.

Everything else is in the menu: Ctrl+Space inside Force.

forceOpen the cockpit.
force new NAMEStart a session in the current folder.
force importList sessions Force doesn't manage yet; force import ID takes one over.
force host add NAMEAdd another machine you reach with ssh.
force settingsShow settings; force settings set KEY VALUE changes one.
force updateInstall the newest release. Sessions keep running.
force uninstallRemove Force; your conversations stay.
